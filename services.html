What We Offer

Four Ways to Bring Santa to You

However you'd like to celebrate, there's a real-beard Santa visit to match — pick the one that fits, or ask us to help you plan something custom.

🏠

Home & Private Visits

Bring Santa right into your living room. This is the most personal way to experience the magic — a quiet, unhurried visit where Santa reads stories, checks the "nice list," takes photos by the tree, and helps little ones write (or share) their letters.

  • Perfect for families with young children
  • Flexible timing to fit your evening
  • Bring milk, cookies, and your family's own traditions
  • Great for small gatherings with extended family or close friends
Request a Home Visit
🏢

Corporate & Community Events

From office holiday parties to town tree lightings, school breakfasts, and community festivals — Santa brings warmth and a little bit of wonder to any crowd, large or small, across the Capital District.

  • Tree lightings, festivals, and downtown holiday events
  • Corporate parties and employee appreciation events
  • School and community organization gatherings
  • Photo-friendly and comfortable working a room
Request Santa for an Event
📸

Photo Sessions

A dedicated Santa photo experience — whether at your venue, a local studio, or an outdoor Capital District backdrop. Great for families, kids, and even the family dog, with real whiskers and no fake beard in sight.

  • On-location or studio sessions available
  • Mini-sessions for quick, classic Santa photos
  • Great for families, groups, and pets
  • Ideal for holiday cards and keepsake portraits
Request a Photo Session
💻

Virtual Visits

Can't be there in person? Santa can still be part of the moment with a live video call — a personalized visit, a bedtime message, or a surprise shoutout for a grandchild, niece, or nephew near or far.

  • Live video call, scheduled at a time that works for you
  • Personalized by name for each child
  • Great for out-of-town family or long-distance surprises
  • No travel required, from anywhere
Request a Virtual Visit
