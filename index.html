Our Story

The Beard Is Real. So Is the Magic.

There's something different about a Santa with a genuine, snow-white beard — no elastic, no itch, no giveaway. Believe in the Beard was founded to bring that unmistakable authenticity to families and communities across the Capital District, creating moments that feel true, warm, and truly magical.

Whether it's a quiet visit by the tree, a lively community tree lighting, or a video call to a grandchild far from home, every visit is grounded in the same thing: genuine Christmas spirit, delivered with care.

About Santa Jay

The Man Behind the Beard

When Jay isn't spreading real Christmas magic across the Capital District, he's home in Rotterdam, NY with the people (and pups) who matter most.

He's been married to his wife Julie for 13 years, and together they're raising two boys, Casey (11) and Austin (9) — plus two very good dogs, Lacey and Otis.

That same warmth Jay brings home every day is exactly what he brings to every visit, every event, and every family he meets as Santa.
